## Scope and integrity

Vendor datasheets, CAD files, logos, and photographs were intentionally omitted
because their rights may differ from the covered source selected here.

`dataset/manifest.json` records source URLs, hashes, roles, byte sizes, page
counts, and derivation metadata for the distributed assets. It is the
authoritative machine-readable inventory.

Distribution of this curated corpus does not imply endorsement by DTU, the
OpenLPBF contributors, or CERN.
